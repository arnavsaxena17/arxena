Job titles are messy. “CHRO”, “Head of People”, and “VP HR” can mean the same seat. Arxena collapses them into a **short controlled vocabulary**, then uses that vocabulary for people search, org charts, and agents.

The pattern is the same shape as a sector → industry hierarchy in finance — or Apollo’s short seniority list — not a public dump of every mapped title:

<TaxonomyShapeDiagram />

<Note>
Public APIs expose flat grade constants and a nested **labels-only** function tree (`GET /people-api/taxonomy/tree`: root → functions). Assignment engines, Boolean generators, and raw truth-tree operators stay private.
</Note>

| Noun | Meaning | Public altitude |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Function root** | Department family (e.g. human resources, engineering) | Nested tree parent + flat constants |
| **Function** | Role family under a root | Nested under roots in `/taxonomy/tree` |
| **Grade** | Seniority (entry / mid / leadership) | Flat label list / dropdown |
| **Grade category** | Org-chart / leadership band | Chart layout + illustrative examples — not scoring math |

**Publish the nouns. Protect the verbs.** Labels and one worked NL example are the product surface. Assignment engines, full title maps, and Boolean generators stay internal.

## How a query works (outcomes only)

<TaxonomyQueryFlow />

1. You (or Claude via MCP) send a **natural-language** role query and a company.
2. Arxena resolves synonyms and company-relative seniority **server-side**.
3. You get people plus a small `resolved` / `resolved_as` object to sanity-check — not a walkthrough of how the match was made.

Primary path: [Natural language to people at a company](/cookbooks/nl-to-people-at-company).

## Public constants (ungated)

Short flat lists plus a nested function label tree — enough to drive selects, not enough to rebuild the engines:

```bash
curl https://app.arxena.com/people-api/taxonomy/constants
curl https://app.arxena.com/people-api/taxonomy/tree
```

See [List taxonomy constants](/api-reference/taxonomy/list-taxonomy-constants) and [List taxonomy tree](/api-reference/taxonomy/list-taxonomy-tree).

## What stays private

| Keep internal | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Booltree operators (`and` / `or` / `not`) | Assignment engine |
| Boolean / keyword generation details | Paid capability + moat |
| Scoring, thresholds, tie-breaks | Assignment engine |
| Correction / feedback flywheel mechanics | Durable advantage |

Authenticated classify/browse endpoints exist for integrators who already know codes. Prefer NL resolve for products and agents. Fair use: [Authentication](/api-reference/authentication#fair-use).

## Org charts

The product org chart is a **view** over the same vocabulary. Query “CHRO” in the Title query bar to filter/highlight — same resolution chips, no second ontology. “Instantly queryable” means cached charts are fast; first-time companies may take a short live build (see `resolved` / freshness on chart payloads when present).
