## Intent glossary

What clients say against what they mean. Each entry has a default; the exception says when to ask or deviate. Not in this list means your own reading is fine. Add entries when a real request is misread, with the worked example.

### Says → means

| Client says | Default | Exception / when to ask |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Sales Rep", "AE", "BDR", "field sales" | Recruiting for that role | A leadership title that could be either: ask |
| "India companies" | Headquartered in India | "Operating in", "present in" or "selling into" India: presence |
| "Mumbai" (or any city) | The city and its metro | Never widen to the country |
| "Tier 1 cities" | Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune, Kolkata, Ahmedabad | The client lists their own: use theirs |
| "MD" | Managing director | Healthcare request: medical doctor. Ask when both fit |
| "Leaders", "decision makers", "senior folks" | Names no role | Ask which roles |
| "Chains", "brands" | Companies with several outlets, by brand | "Outlets" or "stores": places on a map (`places`) |
| "More like these" | Same industry, size band and geography as the seeds | Seeds differ on an attribute: ask which |
| "Doctors", "CAs", other qualified specialists | Experience counts from the qualification date | Date not shown: unknown |
| "Group HR head", "Head of HR" in a promoter-owned group | The person who owns HR policy for the group | May sit at a holding company or report to a family member (`org-structure-insights`) |

Two readings of one word that the table does not settle: ask what it means before spending, likelier reading recommended.
