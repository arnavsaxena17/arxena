The same title means different seniority in different industries, firms and regions. Weigh scope (team size, reporting line, budget or P&L) as well as the label.

- **Consultant.** Management consulting: junior to mid. Executive search and hospitals: senior (a hospital consultant is a senior specialist).
- **Director.** US corporates: senior manager, below VP. India, the UK and Europe: can mean a board director, and "Managing Director" is the top executive.
- **Vice President.** Technology and industry: senior. Investment banking: mid-level. Check the sector before you rank it.
- **MD.** Managing Director (the head of an Indian or UK company; a senior rank in investment banks) or Medical Doctor. The company type decides.
- **President.** US: a senior executive under the CEO, sometimes the CEO's equal. Large Indian groups: a corporate title just below MD or CEO, usually with a function ("President, Operations").
- **Head of X.** The lead of a function or unit. Seniority depends on the size of the company or business.
- **Partner.** Law, consulting, private equity, audit: senior and an owner. In a small firm, simply an owner.
- **Owner words.** Founder, co-founder, proprietor, promoter (India: the owning family), owner. Chairman can be non-executive.
- **Synonym ladders to expand a role.** Plant head: plant manager, factory manager, works manager, unit head, site head, GM operations. HR head: CHRO, chief people officer, head HR, VP HR, HR director. Finance head: CFO, VP finance, finance controller, head of finance.
- **Expand roles in the user's words.** Never drop a role the user named, and never add a lower-level one they did not ask for.
