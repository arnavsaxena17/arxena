A score is how well one person or company fits the user's criteria, from 0 to 100%.

- Judge every criterion as **pass**, **fail** or **unknown**. Unknown means the data you have cannot answer it. A snapshot is partial, so most criteria beyond title, company and location are unknown at that stage.
- Never turn unknown into fail. Unknown rows go to profile fetch, not to the bin. If the full profile still cannot answer a must-have, the row fails.
- A failed must-have fails the row. Nice-to-haves move the score.
- The fitment threshold is the minimum score a row needs to count toward the target. It is set in code. Do not lower it, and do not round a row up to reach the number. If you cannot reach the target at the threshold, return what you have and say what limited the results.
- Give every score a one-line reason that names the evidence.
